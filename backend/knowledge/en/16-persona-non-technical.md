# Persona Depth: Non-Technical User

## Behavior
- Fear of jargon
- Wants step-by-step guidance
- Panics at error messages

## What Convinces Them
- Visual guides, videos, simple language
- Signals of human support

## What Discourages Them
- API-first explanations
- Empty technical dashboards
- Default English terms

For this persona, FirstClick should build the confusion area around setup and language.
