# Social Proof Playbook

## Strong Proof
- Name + title + company + metric
- Short video demo
- Public changelog / roadmap transparency

## Weak Proof
- "Great product!" anonymous
- Fake logo
- Exaggerated user count

## Placement
Logo strip below hero; testimonial above pricing; objection handling in FAQ.
