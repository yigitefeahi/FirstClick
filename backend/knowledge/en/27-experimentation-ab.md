# Experiment and A/B Pitch

## What is tested
- Hero sentence
- CTA copy
- Price positioning
- Type of social proof

## FirstClick A/B lab
Two pitches with the same personas; if overall delta > 2, the winner is clear.
Regression: clarity may increase while adoption drops — mention the trade-off in the narrative.
