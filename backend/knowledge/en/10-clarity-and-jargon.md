# Clarity, Jargon, and Comprehensibility

If the clarityScore is low, there is usually a language problem.

## Jargon Red List
AI-powered, seamless, robust, scalable, next-gen, disrupt, synergy, leverage, holistic — these do not carry meaning on their own.

## Good Explanation Formula
[Who] + [task] + [result] + [proof/time]
Example: "Reduces candidate screening for hiring teams from 30 min to 8 min — 12 company case."

## Feature → Benefit Translation
- Feature: "Automatic tagging"
- Benefit: "You stop classifying candidates manually"

## Persona
Non-technical and first-timer personas are heavily penalized by jargon.
