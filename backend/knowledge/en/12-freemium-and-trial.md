# Freemium and Trial Design

## Freemium Best Practices
- Real value in the free plan (not just a demo)
- Limit is clear and fair
- Upgrade moment coincides with value moment

## Trial Best Practices
- Card is optional, preference must be stated
- Trial end warning
- Data is not deleted; user is downgraded
