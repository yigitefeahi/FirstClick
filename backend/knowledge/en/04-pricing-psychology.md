# Pricing Psychology and Transparency

Price clarity is critical for price-sensitive personas and general adoption.

## Transparency
- If the pricing page cannot be found or "contact sales" is the only option, the score drops for SMBs/startups.
- Free plan limits must be clear: user / project / message.
- Hidden fees (seat, overage) cause skeptical + price-sensitive drop-off.

## Anchoring and Packages
- 3 classic packages: Free / Pro / Business.
- If the middle package is visually highlighted, selection becomes easier.
- Annual discount of 15–20% is typical; "2 months free" is a clear expression.

## Trial
- 14 days is common; whether a card is required or not should be specified.
- If a card is required, note the friction at firstImpression.
- Easy cancellation is a trust signal.

## Persona
- student: education discount / free tier.
- busy-professional: ROI and time-saving language.
- skeptical: money-back / SLA.
