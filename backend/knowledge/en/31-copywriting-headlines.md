## Extended Expert Notes

The analyst reads CTA signals together. If there is a hero promise in the product corpus, a contradiction is sought with the UI/value proposition; if not, the [kb] general rule is applied. In persona reaction, CTA friction is written to the timeline. Concrete suggestion: reduce the hero text to a single sentence, provide visual proof for the value proposition, add micro-assurance for the CTA. Score impact: sensitive to clarity and adoption landing quality; onboardingRisk increases in the absence of a value proposition.
