# Marketplace (two-sided) dynamics

In the Marketplace package, buyers and sellers are considered as separate personas.

## Cold start
- If one side is full and the other is empty, there is no value.
- Early incentive: guaranteed demand / seed supply.

## Trust
- Verification, escrow, rating.
- First-timer: "Will I get scammed?"

## Price and commission
- Commission should be transparent.
- Hidden fee = price-sensitive drop-off.

## Landing
- Two CTAs ("Buy" / "Sell") should be clearly separated; a mixed hero confuses users.

## Extended expert notes

The analyst reads CTA signals together. If there is a hero promise in the product corpus, a contradiction with the UI/value proposition is sought; if not, the [kb] general rule is applied. In persona reactions, CTA friction is written to the timeline. Concrete suggestion: reduce the hero text to a single sentence, provide visual proof for the value proposition, add micro-assurance for the CTA. Score impact: clarity and adoption are sensitive to landing quality; onboardingRisk increases if the value proposition is lacking.
