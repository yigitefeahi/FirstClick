# Product Metrics and Score Interpretation

## How to Read FirstClick Scores
- overall: general first impression health
- clarity: comprehensibility
- adoption: willingness to use
- onboardingRisk: high = bad (inverse)
- targetFit: audience fit

## Thresholds (rough)
- 70+ healthy
- 45–69 mixed; action required
- <45 critical rewrite

A +10 overall improvement in the lab is considered meaningful.
