# Trust Signals and Proof

The skeptical persona is the main type that lowers the score in FirstClick. If there is no trust signal, the likelihood is Low.

## Types of Proof
1. Customer logos (recognizable brand)
2. Numerical results (metrics)
3. Third-party review (G2, Product Hunt)
4. Security (SOC2, KVKK, GDPR) — especially B2B
5. Human face / founder story (early stage)

## The Marketing Language Trap
- "Revolutionary", "world's best" → triggers a skeptical reaction.
- Concrete: "average setup 4 minutes", "NPS 62".

## Security and Privacy
- Where is the data? Who can access it? Right to deletion?
- In B2B, these questions fall under toughQuestions.

## FirstClick Rule
If there is no proof in the corpus, the persona should say "I didn't see it"; do not write a fabricated case study. Use [kb:trust] for the general rule, and [doc:…] for product-specific proof.
