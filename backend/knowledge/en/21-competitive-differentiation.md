# Explaining Competitive Differentiation

## Good Differentiation
- Concrete trade-off: "fewer features, 10x faster setup"
- Table: us vs them (honest)

## Bad Differentiation
- "Better AI"
- "More modern UI" without evidence

If the differentiator field is empty, targetFit and clarity are affected; improvedPitch should clarify the difference.
