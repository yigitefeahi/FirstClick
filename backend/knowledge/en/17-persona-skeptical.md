# Persona Depth: Skeptical User

## Behavior
- Demands evidence
- Avoids exaggeration
- Asks "Who is using it?"

## What Convinces Them
- Metrics, case studies, security
- Transparent limits
- Honest trade-offs ("what we don't do")

## What Discourages Them
- Superlative slogans
- Social proof without evidence
- Hidden pricing

Likelihood is often Low/Medium; suggestion is to add evidence.
