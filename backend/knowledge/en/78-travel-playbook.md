# Travel Sector Playbook

This file gains importance in FirstClick analysis when the sector package **travel** is selected. Each section can be retrieved as an independent RAG piece; each section contains the sector name. Do not write fabricated statistics, conversion rates, or definitive regulatory provisions. Heuristics are clearly marked.

## Scope and Business Model Context (travel)

Travel products may include accommodation, flights, tours, experiences, business travel, or meta-search. Revenue can be commission, markup, or subscription. Price transparency, cancellation policy, and trust (verification, reviews) are critical. If there is a host–guest duality, marketplace signals are also sought. If "cheapest" is unsubstantiated, skeptical toughQuestions apply.

## First Impression Questions (travel)

What is being reserved? When is the total price shown? Cancellation flexibility? Is membership mandatory? Mobile booking quality? Price-sensitive wants to avoid surprise fees; skeptical worries about fake reviews; busy wants fast checkout.

## Onboarding and Activation (travel)

Activation: first search, saved trip, or reservation. Mandatory membership before search can weaken adoption. Empty results should suggest alternative dates/locations. Host onboarding should be handled separately.

## Price, Trust, and Compliance Warnings (travel)

Mandatory fees appearing at the last step cause distrust. Cancellation summary should be visible. Identity verification must be justified. Exaggerated "sold out" should be stock-dependent. Local tax breakdown.

## Persona Reactions (travel)

Price-sensitive: cleaning/service fee. Skeptical: visual-location mismatch. Busy: long form. Family first-timer: fear of cancellation. Student: lack of filters.

## Drop-off Timeline Patterns (travel)

Empty search → hidden fee → membership wall → 3DS friction → unclear voucher. Stages: Search, Results, Checkout, Post-booking.

## Evidence to Seek in Corpus (travel)

Price breakdown, cancellation, reviews, verification, map, fees, membership, support, mobile, host panel.

## FirstClick Score Effects (travel)

Clarity: reservation object. Adoption: search→payment friction. OnboardingRisk: membership/host setup. Trust: fees+cancellation+reviews.

## Action Checklist (travel)

1) Show total price early. 2) Cancellation summary. 3) Search without membership. 4) Alternatives for empty results. 5) Remove fake scarcity. 6) Fee breakdown. 7) Clear voucher. 8) Support. 9) Separate host/guest. 10) Quote.

## Total Price and Breakdown (travel)

In travel, showing a low per night/per person price and inflating the total is a classic distrust pattern. Tax, cleaning, baggage, and service fees should be visible early. FirstClick does not fabricate conversion rates. If "cheapest" is unsubstantiated, toughQuestions apply. Instant price changes should be explained.

## Cancellation, Membership, and Post-booking (travel)

If the flexible cancellation promise is full of exceptions, it's a contradiction. Mandatory membership before search weakens adoption. If post-booking voucher/QR is unclear, busy-professional friction arises. Host onboarding should be handled separately and not mixed with the guest flow.

## Package, Meta-search, and Loyalty (travel)

If there is no component price breakdown in flight+hotel packages, price-sensitive distrust arises. If meta-search "best" ranking includes sponsored results, it must be labeled; otherwise, skeptical toughQuestions apply. If loyalty point usage conditions are hidden, it's a contradiction. If accessibility (mobility needs) filters are missing, it's a missing signal for a11y personas — do not claim mandatory features.

If group booking, child age pricing, and binding flight rules are unclear in microcopy, confusion arises. If travel insurance upsell is hard to opt out of, it's a dark pattern. If local experience/tour cancellation weather policy is unclear, first-timer anxiety increases.

## Business Travel and Policy Engine (travel)

In bleisure/business travel products, if company policy (cabin class, hotel price cap) is unclear, economic buyer adoption is delayed. If there is no approval flow (manager approve), B2B promise is weak. Invoice/receipt export may be critical for accounting. Employee self-book and travel manager panel should not be mixed. If carbon/offset claims are unsubstantiated, skeptical toughQuestions apply — do not fabricate metrics.

If the disruption help line is only an FAQ, it's friction. Lounge/priority add-on surprise prices should be shown early at checkout.

## User Journeys (travel)

In FirstClick travel analysis, guest, host, and (if any) travel manager journeys are separated.

**Guest reservation path:** Search (preferably without membership) → results list/map → PDP (total price+cancellation) → checkout (fee breakdown, 3DS) → voucher/QR/check-in → support/cancellation. Drop-off: empty results, hidden fee, membership wall, unclear voucher, fake scarcity.

**Host / business path:** Registration → verification → calendar/pricing → first listing → reservation acceptance → payout. Mixed onboarding with guest flow reduces clarity↓.

**Business travel path:** Policy cap → search filter → manager approval → invoice export. If no approval, B2B promise is weak.

**Meta-search path:** Comparison → redirect to external site; look for sponsored ranking label.

Timeline: Search, Results, Checkout, Post-booking, Host setup, Policy approval. Package (flight+hotel) component breakdown is examined separately.

## Persona Conflicts (travel)

- **Price-sensitive vs mandatory fees:** Low nightly price + cleaning/service added later → distrust. Show total early.
- **Skeptical vs marketing scarcity:** If "last room" is not stock-based, it's a manipulation signal; look for stock link.
- **Busy vs long form / membership:** Wants fast checkout; mandatory membership lowers adoption↓.
- **Family first-timer vs cancellation uncertainty:** Flexible promise + exception maze creates fear; summary card is a heuristic.
- **Student vs lack of filters:** If there is no budget/student filter, it's a missing signal — do not fabricate market share.
- **Host vs guest trust:** Host wants verification; guest wants frictionless entry. Verification must be justified.
- **Economic buyer (business) vs bleisure:** If no policy engine, adoption is delayed; if carbon claim is unsubstantiated, toughQuestions apply.

## Action Descriptions (travel)

1) **Early total:** Estimated total or "total ≈" + breakdown tooltip on result card.
2) **Cancellation summary:** 2–3 line summary + policy link on PDP; do not hide exceptions.
3) **Search without membership:** Registration at checkout or favorite moment.

5) **Honest scarcity:** If not stock-based, remove or link to stock.

9) **Meta label:** Mark sponsored/advertised results.
10) **Insurance:** Opt-in clear; if opt-out is hard, note dark pattern.
